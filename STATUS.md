# Program status

**Edition prepared for review · 3 October 2026**

The public presentation brings the information and phase constructions together with the computational and physical laboratories. The scope of each contribution is recorded here so reviewers can select a concrete object.

| Area | Available contribution | Next review target |
|---|---|---|
| Information and closure | Definitions, non-factorization criterion, sufficient boundary descriptions | Generality of contracts and contextual equivalence |
| Reflexive geometry | Proof of $J^2=-I$, a minimal invariant plane, four-phase orbit | Selection and interpretation of the hypotheses |
| Phase and measure | Product representation with 16 signatures and normalized invariant weights $1/16$, $15/16$ | Realization of independent phase actions and physical measure |
| Composition | Exact relational reclosure, with boundary variables retained | Efficient representations and proof systems |
| Computation | Explicit truncated-counter family and a certified-descent research target | Resource bounds on new constraint families |
| Navier–Stokes | Exact material identities, model benchmark, derived JHTDB tables | Independent instantaneous PDE budget and sampling provenance |
| Yang–Mills | Exact $SU(2)$ commutator observable and a finite Gaussian control | Uniform coercivity and comparison with physical dynamics |
| Physical scale | Conditional chain from quadratic weight to rate and length ratios | Derive the observable map and system-specific selection |

The proofs and calculations supplied with the program support the stated scopes. Independent specialist review is invited through [REVIEW.md](REVIEW.md). The JHTDB tables retain their documented sampling and derivative sensitivity; the Gaussian control is a finite linearized model calculation. Physical regularity, complexity, and continuum gap questions are research objectives of their respective branches.
