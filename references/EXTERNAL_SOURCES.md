Public-status note for the September 2026 OpenAI Navier–Stokes announcement:

- OpenAI released a manuscript and Lean formalization on 8 September 2026.

## Academic positioning sources

The positioning document `publication/academic_context_and_research_interfaces.md` compares the program, without claiming equivalence, with:

- relational quantum mechanics and relational-clock approaches;
- Machian/Barbour-type relational dynamics;
- ontic structural realism;
- operational/informational reconstructions of quantum theory;
- Lawvere-style self-reference and fixed-point mathematics;
- information geometry;
- geometric and Lagrangian vorticity dynamics, including direction-coherence criteria, quaternionic Euler dynamics, viscous tilting, twist/anti-twist, Burgers-vortex and restricted-Euler benchmarks.

The purpose of those citations is to identify formal languages, known constraints, and testable interfaces—not to claim that those frameworks derive or endorse the repository's ontology.
